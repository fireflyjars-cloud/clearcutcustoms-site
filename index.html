Our Story

Clear Cut Window Tinting is a husband-and-wife–owned business built on nearly three decades of marriage, craftsmanship, and commitment to excellence. We've been in the window tint and paint protection film industry since the late 1990s — earning industry recognition, winning awards, and training tinters across the Front Range and around the country.

After years of growth and visibility, we made a deliberate decision: not to grow bigger, but to grow better. Today, Clear Cut operates as a private, boutique studio at our home, by appointment only, where we focus on fewer jobs, deeper relationships, and exceptional results — for residential, commercial, and automotive clients alike, throughout Aurora, Denver, Parker, Bennett, and Strasburg.

Our legacy continues through family — Clear Cut 2.0, located in Colorado Springs, is owned and operated by our son and daughter, carrying forward the same values and craftsmanship that built the Clear Cut name.

Clear Cut is right for you if you:

  • Want protected, long-term results for your home, business, or vehicle
  • Value precise, unhurried craftsmanship
  • Appreciate privacy and a relaxed, appointment-only experience
  • Choose quality and longevity over shortcuts

We may not be the right fit if you:

  • Need same-day service or walk-in availability
  • Are primarily price-shopping rather than value-focused
  • Prefer a high-traffic commercial shop environment
