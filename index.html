Our Story

Clear Cut Window Tinting is a husband-and-wife–owned business built on nearly three decades of marriage, craftsmanship, and commitment to excellence. We've been in the window tint and paint protection film industry since the late 1990s — earning industry recognition, winning awards, and training tinters across the Front Range and around the country.

After years of growth and visibility, we made a deliberate decision: not to grow bigger, but to grow better. Today, Clear Cut operates as a boutique studio out of our home, where we focus on fewer jobs, deeper relationships, and exceptional results — and where scheduling is simple, with same-day appointments often available. Automotive tint and paint protection film are our primary focus, drawing clients to our Aurora studio from across the Front Range, the Eastern Plains, and even the Western Slope — alongside residential and commercial window tinting we bring directly to homes and businesses throughout Aurora, Denver, Parker, Centennial, and Strasburg.

Our legacy continues through family — Clear Cut 2.0, located in Colorado Springs, is owned and operated by our son and daughter, carrying forward the same values and craftsmanship that built the Clear Cut name.
